Services

Controller- and CFO-level financial leadership delivered in a practical, flexible way. We support audit readiness but do not perform or sign audit engagements.

1

Financial Health Reviews

A clear, structured look at your Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, account reconciliations, cash flow, and the processes behind the numbers. You leave with a realistic picture of where things stand and prioritized recommendations.

2

Accounting Operations Support

Hands-on help stabilizing and improving day-to-day accounting operations — close processes, reconciliations, reporting accuracy, and the workflows that keep the books clean and decision-ready.

3

Treasury & Cash Management

Cash forecasting, liquidity planning, banking controls, and stronger oversight of funds flow. Designed for businesses that need reliable visibility into cash without building a full internal treasury function.

4

Financial Process Documentation

Clear documentation of key financial processes so your team (and any future hires or advisors) can operate consistently. Reduces key-person risk and makes improvements stick.

5

Cleanup & Stabilization Projects

Targeted projects to clean up the books, resolve long-standing issues, strengthen reconciliations, and put sustainable processes in place so the numbers can be trusted going forward.

6

Internal Controls & Risk Reviews

Practical review of internal controls and financial risk points. We identify gaps, strengthen oversight, and help create an environment that supports accurate reporting and audit readiness.

Engagements are structured as fixed-fee assessments, fixed-fee cleanup projects, or recurring monthly advisory retainers.
