Services

Financial operations support that improves accuracy and confidence.

Outsourced accounting operations, treasury support, cleanup projects, and financial diagnostics for growing companies. We support audit readiness—but we do not perform or sign audits.

Financial health reviews

  • Reconciliation and process gap assessment
  • Clear priorities: what to fix first and why

Cleanup & stabilization projects

  • Historical financial cleanup
  • Cash-to-accrual conversions that reflect operations
  • Inventory, COGS, and margin normalization
  • Balance sheet substantiation and documentation

Accounting operations support

  • Month-end close design and oversight
  • Reporting packages owners can actually use
  • Fractional controller support on a defined scope

Treasury & cash management

  • Cash visibility and forecasting discipline
  • Working capital and payment process review
  • Controls around cash movement and approvals

Process documentation

  • Close checklists and recurring calendars

  • Handoff-ready SOPs so results are repeatable

Internal controls & risk reviews

  • Segregation of duties where it matters most
  • Audit and diligence readiness support
