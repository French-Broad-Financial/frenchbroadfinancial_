About French Broad Financial

Practical financial leadership for growing businesses

French Broad Financial is a boutique financial operations advisory firm. We provide outsourced accounting operations, treasury management, process improvement, and financial diagnostics for small and mid-sized businesses.

Our mission is simple: help business owners trust their financial information so they can make better decisions.

Every engagement begins by understanding the business — reviewing the Profit & Loss statement, Balance Sheet, account reconciliations, cash flow, and the processes behind the numbers.

Tyler Easterbrook, Founder

Tyler brings more than nine years of experience across treasury, financial reporting, and accounting operations. He has led teams and built processes in both Fortune 500 and high-growth environments, with deep expertise in cash management, forecasting, internal controls, and ERP implementations.

His background includes:

  • Treasury leadership supporting complex trust and operating accounts
  • Multi-entity accounting and consolidations (including 800+ global entities)
  • Month-end close ownership and financial reporting
  • ERP transitions (QuickBooks → Oracle Cloud, SAP → Sage → NetSuite)
  • Cash forecasting, liquidity planning, and credit line management
  • Building formal controls, playbooks, and reconciliation frameworks

Tyler holds a Master of Accountancy and a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration (Accounting, magna cum laude) from Western Carolina University.
