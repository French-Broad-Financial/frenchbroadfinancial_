About

Built for owners who need to trust their numbers.

French Broad Financial is a boutique financial operations advisory firm serving small and mid-sized businesses—especially across Western North Carolina’s French Broad River region.

Tyler Easterbrook
Founder
tyler.easterbrook@frenchbroadfinancial.com

Founder

Tyler brings accounting leadership experience spanning ERP implementation, intercompany accounting, consolidations, treasury, and management of year-end external audits—alongside people leadership in hiring, coaching, and mentoring finance teams.

French Broad Financial was built around a simple idea: most growing companies don’t have “bad accounting.” They have incomplete, undocumented, or misaligned accounting for the stage they’re entering.

The work focuses on controller-level cleanup, process design, and practical financial leadership—so owners can stop firefighting the books and start managing with clearer information.

Background includes

  • ERP implementation & process design
  • Intercompany accounting & consolidations
  • Treasury & cash operations
  • Year-end external audit management
  • Hiring, coaching, and mentoring finance teams

Mission

Provide practical financial leadership that improves accounting accuracy, strengthens internal controls, and gives business owners confidence in their financial reporting.

Who we serve

Growing companies that need more than bookkeeping.

Typical clients are businesses with roughly 10–100 employees and about $2M–$50M in revenue.

Integrity

Straight answers about what the financials show—and what they don’t.

Accuracy

Numbers that reconcile, processes that hold up, documentation that lasts.

Practical solutions

Recommendations sized for real teams—not enterprise theater.

Client partnership

Clear communication, transparent scope, and work that transfers ownership back to you.
